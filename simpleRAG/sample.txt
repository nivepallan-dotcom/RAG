The manufacturing plant operates three major turbine units: A, B, and C.

Turbine A experienced repeated overheating during peak production hours. 
Investigation revealed that improper lubrication and delayed maintenance checks were the primary causes.

Turbine B showed vibration anomalies caused by rotor imbalance. 
After recalibration, vibration levels dropped by 25 percent.

Turbine C encountered cooling system inefficiencies due to clogged airflow channels.
Cleaning and airflow optimization improved efficiency by 18 percent.

Preventive maintenance strategies across all turbines reduced unexpected downtime by 35 percent.

Monthly inspections are required to ensure lubrication systems and cooling mechanisms operate within safe thresholds.
